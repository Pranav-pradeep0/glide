Glide privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Glide plays the videos and music already on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking for advertising. Your media files never leave your phone. A few features look things up online, and this page lists each one, what it sends and where.

What stays on your phone

Your watch history, resume positions, settings, music queue, and the subtitles and lyrics Glide saves are kept only on your phone. Glide reads your videos and music to play them. It does not upload them. Uninstalling Glide deletes all of this.

What leaves your phone, and why

Requests marked “via Glide's server” go through a small server Glide runs on Cloudflare, which adds the service's access key and passes the request on. That server keeps no record of what you asked for. Its logs hold only which feature was used and whether it worked.

FeatureWhat is sentTo whomWhen
Subtitle search The video's title, file name and, if known, its IMDb id SubDL, via Glide's server. The subtitle file itself downloads straight from SubDL. When you search for subtitles
Haptic feedback The video's title and file name, to find captions that describe sounds SubDL, via Glide's server Automatically when you play a video with haptics on
Movie and show details The title and year worked out from the file name. The poster image then loads straight from the image host OMDb gives. OMDb, via Glide's server When you open a video's details
Lyrics The song's artist, title, album and length LRCLIB (lrclib.net), directly Automatically when a song plays, if “Online lyrics” is on in Settings
Recap (AI summary) The video's title and up to 5,000 characters of its subtitle dialogue up to where you are Groq, an AI provider, via Glide's server Only when you tap Recap
Report this recap The video's title and the recap text Kept on Glide's server for review, then deleted after 180 days Only when you report a recap
App updates An app-specific device id (Android's ANDROID_ID), the app version, phone make and model, Android version, language, time zone, update events, and crash details if the app crashes Stallion (stalliontech.io), the service that delivers Glide's in-app updates When the app starts and when an update is installed
Crash reports The error details, app and Android version, and phone model The Glide developer, by email Only if you press Send after a crash. Your email app opens with the report so you can read it first. You can turn this off in Settings.

Every one of these services sees your phone's IP address when it is contacted, as any website does. Glide's server uses it briefly to stop abuse and does not store it. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

What Glide does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, contacts, location or advertising id.
  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking for advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No uploading of your videos, music or photos.

Permissions

  • Videos and music on your device: to list and play them.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls while media plays.
  • Internet: for the online features listed above.

Children

Glide is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Turn off online lyrics and crash report offers in Settings.
  • Don't use subtitle search, Recap or haptic feedback if you don't want those lookups sent.
  • Clear the subtitle cache in Settings, or uninstall Glide to delete everything it stored.
  • Email us to ask about or delete a recap report you sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
